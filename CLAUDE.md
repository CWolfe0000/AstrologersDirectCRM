# House style

- Headings: capitalize the first letter of every word (Title Case), on every website and screen. This applies to page headings, section and card headings, and browser-tab titles. In `datebyfate/` the stylesheet does this for `h1`–`h4`, `legend` and anything with the `heading` class; write tab titles in Title Case in the source.

# Astrology readings (Datebyfate)

Editorial standard set by the owner, an experienced astrologer, after reviewing readings on their own chart:

- Never read a placement alone. A planet by sign or house is weighed by the aspects it actually makes in that chart, and by whether it rules the chart or the Sun sign. Do not assert a difficulty (a wound, a trust problem) from a placement by itself.
- No stereotypes and no loaded labels about the person ("dangerous", "alarming", "intimidating"). Write from inside their experience, in tendencies, without absolutes or invented biography.
- Product and content decisions are the owner's. Do not leave material out, or restrict how it is framed, on your own judgment: raise it and ask.
